EVTXER - third-party software
=============================

EVTXER starts the programs below as separate programs and reads the files
they write. They are not part of EVTXER and keep their own licences. Each is
shipped unmodified, exactly as its authors released it. The licence texts
are in this folder.

HAYABUSA DETECTION RULES                             code\hayabusa-x64\rules\
------------------------
Licence: Detection Rule License (DRL) 1.1 - DRL-1.1.txt
         (also rules\LICENSE.md, beside the rules).
Authors: named in each rule's "author" field. EVTXER keeps that field: in
         the rules as shipped, and in every detection it uploads
         (rule.author), as the DRL requires.
Source:  https://github.com/Yamato-Security/hayabusa-rules
         (many rules come from Sigma: https://github.com/SigmaHQ/sigma)

Passing EVTXER on
-----------------
Keep this folder with it. The AGPL lets you pass Hayabusa and Takajo on as
long as their licence and source go with them - this folder does both.
